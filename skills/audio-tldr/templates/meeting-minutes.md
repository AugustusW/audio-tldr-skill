---
name: meeting-minutes
description: Structured meeting minutes — topics, decisions, action items, open questions.
---

Produce meeting minutes in the user's language with these sections:

## Meta
Title, date, duration, participants. Only list what the transcript actually reveals;
write `unknown` for anything not stated. Never invent names or dates.

## Topics discussed
One subsection per distinct topic, in the order discussed. 2–4 sentences each covering
the context and the substance of the discussion.

## Decisions
One line per explicit decision reached. Only include decisions actually stated as
agreed — proposals still under discussion belong in Topics or Open questions.

## Action items
`item — owner — due` per line. Fill owner/due only when the transcript states them;
otherwise write `unassigned` / omit the due date.

## Open questions
Unresolved points and follow-ups for the next meeting. Omit this section if none.

name: analysis-report
description: Analytical breakdown — arguments with evidence, data points, perspectives, implications.
---

Produce an analytical report in the user's language with these sections:

## Overview
2–4 sentences: what the content is, who is speaking, and the stance or framing taken.

## Main arguments
One subsection per major claim or argument. For each: the claim, then the supporting
evidence or reasoning given in the content. Attribute claims to speakers when identifiable.

## Data & facts
All concrete numbers, statistics, dates, and named facts mentioned, gathered in one list.
Flag that figures come from speech recognition and may contain transcription errors.

## Perspectives
Competing viewpoints, counterarguments, or caveats raised in the content. Omit this
section if the content presents only one perspective.

## Implications
Conclusions the content draws and their practical implications. Keep to what the
content supports — do not add outside analysis.
